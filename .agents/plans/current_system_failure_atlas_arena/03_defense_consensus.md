Date: 2026-06-11 | Agent Persona: system_synthesizer

## 1. Responses To Red-Team Critique

### Observatory scope

Accepted. Plan D does not authorize a dashboard or generic analytics system.
Every proposed implementation item must cite a reproduced failure that cannot be
measured reliably without it.

### Behavioral oracles

Accepted. Fixtures lock truth, provenance, freshness, boundedness, and
reproducibility contracts. They do not lock incidental ids, response ordering,
or current trace shape unless those are explicitly part of the approved
contract.

### Baseline preservation

Accepted. Current behavior is captured before any repair. F1 and disconnected
QTR behavior are high-priority reproductions, not immediate patches.

### Support correctness

Accepted. Valid ids are necessary but insufficient. Every source-supported gold
claim receives minimal-support labels and exact support checks.

### Determinism and providers

Accepted. Reports separate deterministic provider-free gates, configured
full-quality measurements, degraded-mode measurements, and semantic review.

### Historical scenario preservation

Accepted. Current-architecture scenarios are created separately. Existing stale
scenario assets are inventoried and classified during Plan D, but not modified
by this planning task.

### Cross-client parity

Accepted. Parity means the same normalized backend transaction for equivalent
requests. Obsidian immediate viewer/selection context remains client-owned and
is evaluated as an additional context layer.

### Privacy and deferral

Accepted. Synthetic/testbed fixtures are the default. Every accepted/assigned
failure records impact, owner, dependency, and blocking status.

## 2. Locked Consensus

1. Plan D belongs to Program 1 and precedes compiler or serving architecture
   changes.
2. No production behavior is changed during initial failure capture.
3. Every concern ends as reproduced, disproven, accepted, or assigned.
4. Every reproduced defect has a minimal fixture, boundary diagnosis, and
   evidence bundle.
5. Claim support correctness is distinct from span-id validity.
6. The evaluation suite has development, frozen regression, holdout,
   adversarial, and optional live-vault partitions.
7. Deterministic and LLM/provider-sensitive gates remain separate.
8. Cross-client parity compares normalized backend evidence while preserving
   Obsidian immediate context.
9. The observatory implements only minimum substrate justified by reproduced
   measurement blockers.
10. Programs 2 and 3 cannot redefine Plan-D labels silently; changed contracts
    require an explicit oracle-version decision.
