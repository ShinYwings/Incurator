# Source-Pair Analyst Proposal: Provenance Is A Research Gate

Date: 2026-06-11 | Agent Persona: source_pair_analyst

## 1. Core Logic & Implementation

Every candidate comparison measures whether selected/generated information still
resolves to minimal source evidence. Aggregate answer quality cannot compensate
for lost support, stale derivation, unsupported graph expansion, or rewritten
formula evidence.

## 2. Pros & Cons

Pros: preserves Incurator's strongest differentiator while comparing external
techniques. Cons: candidates without provenance-oriented reference
implementations require extra instrumentation in disposable spikes.
