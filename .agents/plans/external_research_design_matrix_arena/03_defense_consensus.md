# Defense And Consensus: External Research Decision Protocol
Date: 2026-06-11 | Agent Persona: system_synthesizer

## Program Ordering

Plan E begins only after Plan D1 has merged the current-system Failure Atlas,
baseline identities, labeling methods, and experiment/evidence-bundle contract.
External techniques are compared against that measured baseline rather than
against assumptions.

## 1. Defended Principles

- Research remains failure-first because production adoption must solve a real
  Incurator problem.
- Primary sources define claimed mechanisms, but only local reproduction
  establishes an Incurator decision.
- Disposable spikes remain necessary to prevent premature production changes.
- Provenance, direct factual quality, update behavior, and bounded cost remain
  hard constraints.

## 2. Revisions Accepted From Red Team

1. Begin with an architecture-neutral open-question and simple-control review.
2. Freeze validation and holdout fixtures before candidate tuning.
3. Separate dossier author, spike executor, and decision reviewer roles.
4. Scope every decision by failure, route, source type, and tested scale.
5. Split `adopt` into adoption of a **contract/invariant**, never authorization
   for production implementation.
6. Require update/delete and scale-cost evidence or explicitly label the decision
   provisional.
7. Run spikes only on immutable fixture exports; production/testbed state is
   read-only unless a copied disposable database is used.
8. Require final untouched holdout execution and a provenance audit before a
   decision is accepted.

## 3. Locked Consensus

The Program-1 external research process produces:

- a verified primary-source dossier per candidate;
- a frozen benchmark/evaluation contract;
- reproducible comparative spike reports;
- narrowly scoped adopt/benchmark/reject ADRs;
- a downstream specification map;
- a rejected-technique register with revisit triggers.

It produces no production implementation, schema migration, production
dependency, version bump, or release.

## 4. Initial Decision Posture

Adopt as downstream design contracts:

- claim-level provenance and citation evaluation;
- one authoritative query transaction;
- progressive disclosure under explicit token budgets;
- direct factual quality as a non-regression constraint;
- failure-family and task-family reporting.

Benchmark before implementation:

- contextual chunks;
- PPR and graph-guided expansion;
- denoised hierarchy and query-relevant global/DRIFT flows;
- complexity/sufficiency-gated retrieval;
- bounded iterative retrieval;
- selective formula recovery.

Reject as defaults:

- graph-only retrieval;
- automatic semantic entity merge;
- eager unbounded global summarization;
- fixed top-k for every task;
- model-judge-only gates;
- framework wholesale replacement without mechanism-level evidence.

## 4. Vulnerabilities & Flaws Resolved

- Prevented paper claims, aggregate scores, and model judges from becoming
  architecture authority.
- Isolated spikes from production state and separated decision roles.

## 5. Suggested Alternatives Adopted Or Rejected

- Adopted failure-first mechanism comparisons against the merged D1 baseline.
- Rejected production mutations and adoption without holdout/provenance audit.
